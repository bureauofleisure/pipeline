Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

This policy covers the Instagram account @bureauofleisure.co and the small app we use to post to it (Flow Publisher). Both are run by Flow Agency LLC in Brooklyn, New York.

What the app does

Flow Publisher posts photos and captions to our own Instagram account, on a schedule we approve one post at a time. It reads basic details about our own account and the performance of our own posts. That is all it does. It is not offered to anyone else and nobody else can log in to it.

What we collect about you

Nothing. The app does not read comments, messages, followers' profiles or anyone else's data, and it does not use cookies or tracking on this site. When we credit a photographer, designer or venue, we use the name and public Instagram handle they publish themselves.

What we keep

Our own account's access token and post statistics, stored privately so the scheduled posts can run. We do not sell, share or rent any data to anyone.

Credits, corrections and removal

If you are credited in a post, or you appear in one, and want the credit changed or the post taken down, send us a DM on Instagram or email lewie@flow.agency. We will act on it within seven days.

Deleting your data

We hold no personal data about people who use or follow our account. If you believe we do, email lewie@flow.agency with the subject "Data deletion" and we will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

Changes

If this changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
